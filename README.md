# The Daily Word

A daily devotional page grounded in the Revised Common Lectionary and the Lutheran Confessions (Book of Concord).

Each entry pairs a verse from the current week's appointed readings with a short teaching that retells or reflects on the passage, and — where it fits — a supporting citation from the Augsburg Confession, Luther's Small or Large Catechism, or another confessional document.

## Sources

- Readings: [Revised Common Lectionary](https://lectionary.library.vanderbilt.edu/)
- Confessional citations: [Book of Concord](https://bookofconcord.org/) (public-domain Triglotta translation)
